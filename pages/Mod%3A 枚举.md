## EXPLOSION_TRIGGER_TYPE

These values are used by ExplosionComponent _trigger_ property to determine when the explosion defined on this component will trigger. 

  * ON_DEATH - triggers when the entity housing ExplosionComponent is killed
  * ON_CREATE - triggers the moment ExplosionComponent is added to the entity
  * ON_TIMER - triggers according to the _timeout_frames_ property in the ExplosionComponent
